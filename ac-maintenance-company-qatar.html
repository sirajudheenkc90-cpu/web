AGM is a Qatar AC maintenance company offering annual maintenance contracts (AMC) for both homes and businesses — with scheduled servicing, deep cleaning, repairs and priority support that keep your units efficient, reliable and running longer.

Why an AMC pays off in Qatar

Air conditioning in Qatar works almost year-round, and dust, heat and constant runtime take their toll. An annual maintenance contract replaces reactive, expensive breakdown call-outs with a planned schedule that keeps units clean, efficient and dependable. You get lower energy bills, fewer failures, a longer equipment life and one number to call when something needs attention.

Residential AMC — homes & villas

For villas and apartments, our home AMC keeps your family comfortable and your bills in check. Each visit covers filter and coil cleaning, gas and drainage checks, electrical inspection and a performance test — with priority booking if a unit ever stops cooling.

  • Scheduled visits: two to four services a year, timed around peak season.
  • Deep cleaning: filters, coils and drainage to keep air clean and cooling strong.
  • Priority repairs: faster response and discounted call-outs for AMC customers.
  • All unit types: split, ducted, concealed and multi-zone systems.

Commercial AMC — offices, retail & industry

For businesses, our commercial AMC protects uptime and compliance. We tailor the visit frequency to your equipment and criticality, and can extend cover to chillers, AHUs, FCUs and ventilation under a single agreement. For larger portfolios, see our dedicated commercial HVAC & MEP maintenance and chiller maintenance pages.

  • Planned preventive maintenance (PPM): scheduled to your assets and load.
  • 24/7 breakdown cover: priority response for critical cooling.
  • Reporting: visit logs and asset condition reports for facilities teams.
  • Comprehensive or non-comprehensive: parts included or billed separately.
