AC Gas (Refrigerant) Leak in Qatar: Signs, Risks & Repair

Refrigerant isn't consumed — if your AC is low, it's leaking. Here's how to spot a leak, why 'top-ups' don't last, and how a proper leak repair is done.

  3. AC Gas / Refrigerant Leak

Air conditioners don't use up refrigerant — a system that's 'low on gas' has a leak somewhere. Signs include weak cooling, ice on the copper pipe or indoor coil, longer run times, and a hissing sound. Topping up without finding the leak is a temporary fix that fails within weeks.

Signs of a refrigerant leak

Reduced cooling, ice forming on the suction line or evaporator, the unit running non-stop, higher bills, and sometimes a faint hiss near the pipework or a bubbling sound. Leaks commonly occur at flare joints, corroded coils and valves.

Why top-ups don't last

Adding gas without repairing the leak just delays the problem — the new charge leaks out too, cooling fades again, and running low stresses the compressor. It also wastes refrigerant and money.

Proper leak repair

We pressure-test and use electronic/UV leak detection to find the exact point, repair or replace the leaking component, pull a vacuum to remove air and moisture, then weigh in the precise refrigerant charge to spec.

Prevent future leaks

Regular servicing catches corrosion and loose joints early, and keeps the system at the correct charge. Ask about our AMC plans. Related: AC gas refilling.
