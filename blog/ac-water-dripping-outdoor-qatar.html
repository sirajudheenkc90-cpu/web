Water Dripping from AC Outdoor Unit in Qatar: Normal or a Fault?

Some water at the outdoor unit is normal; a lot, or water indoors, is not. Here's how to tell the difference and when to act.

  3. Water Dripping from Outdoor Unit

A little water at the outdoor unit can be normal condensation, especially in Qatar's humidity — but heavy dripping, water pooling, or leaks indoors point to a drainage or refrigerant issue. The key is where the water is and how much.

When it's normal

In humid weather the outdoor unit can shed some condensate, and during a heat-pump defrost cycle in winter it's normal to see water. A light, occasional drip usually isn't a concern.

When it's a fault

Steady heavy dripping, water pooling around the unit, ice that melts and floods, or any water leaking from the indoor unit indicates a blocked drain, a frozen coil (low refrigerant or airflow), or an install/slope problem.

Safe checks

Look for ice on the pipework or coil (a sign of low refrigerant/airflow), and check the condensate drain isn't blocked. If water is coming indoors, switch the unit off to protect the property.
