AC Water Leaking Indoors in Qatar: Causes and How to Stop It

Water dripping from the indoor unit or staining your ceiling is a warning sign — not just a nuisance. Here's why AC units leak in Qatar and how to fix it before it damages your property.

An AC leaks water when the condensate it normally drains away has nowhere to go. The most common causes are a blocked or algae-clogged drain line, a frozen evaporator coil that melts and overflows, a cracked or misaligned drain pan, or a unit installed without the correct slope. In Qatar's humidity, units produce a lot of condensate, so a small drainage fault quickly becomes a visible leak.

Where the water comes from

Cooling pulls moisture out of your indoor air; that condensate collects in a pan and drains outside. Anything that interrupts this path — a clogged drain, a full pan, ice on the coil — sends water into your ceiling or down the wall instead.

Common causes of AC water leaks

  • Blocked condensate drain: dust, algae and biofilm clog the drain line — the most common cause here.
  • Frozen evaporator coil: low refrigerant or weak airflow ices the coil, which then melts and overflows the pan.
  • Cracked or rusted drain pan: older units develop pan leaks.
  • Incorrect installation slope: if the unit or drain isn't pitched correctly, water pools and backs up.
  • Disconnected or loose drain fittings: vibration works joints loose over time.

What you can safely do now

  • Switch the unit off to stop further water and protect ceilings/electronics.
  • If you can see ice on the indoor unit, leave it off to thaw.
  • Place a container to catch drips, and clear anything valuable from below.

Clearing the drain line, refrigerant work and pan repairs should be done by a technician.

How an AGM engineer fixes it

We locate the source, flush and clear the condensate drain, treat it to stop algae regrowth, repair or replace a damaged pan, correct the slope if the install is wrong, and check refrigerant and airflow if the coil was freezing.
