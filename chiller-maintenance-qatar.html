Why chiller maintenance matters in Qatar

Chillers are usually the single largest energy consumer in a commercial building, and in Qatar's climate they run hard for most of the year. Fouled tubes, low refrigerant charge, poor water treatment or scaling quietly erode efficiency — driving up electricity bills and pushing the machine toward an unexpected failure at the worst possible time. Planned maintenance keeps the plant at design performance and catches problems while they are still small.

What our chiller service covers

  • Preventive maintenance: condenser and evaporator inspection, tube cleaning, refrigerant and oil checks.
  • Electrical & controls: compressor, starter, sensor and safety-device testing.
  • Water-side care: water treatment, condenser water and cooling-tower checks, scale and corrosion control.
  • Efficiency optimisation: performance logging and tuning to restore design kW/ton.
  • Breakdown repair: priority diagnosis and repair of chiller faults.
  • All chiller types: scroll, screw and centrifugal, air-cooled and water-cooled.

Plant we maintain

We service chillers and associated pumps, cooling towers and condenser water systems in offices and towers, malls and retail, hotels, hospitals and clinics, factories and warehouses, and district-cooling connected buildings across Doha and the wider Qatar region.

Contract or one-off

Chiller maintenance can be arranged as part of an annual maintenance contract (AMC), folded into a wider commercial HVAC maintenance agreement, or booked as a one-off service or repair visit.
