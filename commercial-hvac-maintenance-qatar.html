AGM provides commercial HVAC and MEP maintenance contracts (AMC) for buildings across Qatar — combining scheduled preventive maintenance, priority breakdown response and documented compliance reporting to keep your cooling, ventilation and building services running reliably and efficiently.

Maintenance built for facility managers

Downtime, energy waste and surprise repair bills are the three things a facility manager can't afford. An AGM maintenance contract addresses all three: a fixed schedule of planned visits catches faults before they become failures, priority call-out gets critical systems back online fast, and clear reporting gives you the paper trail you need for landlords, auditors and budgets.

What's covered

  • Planned preventive maintenance (PPM): scheduled visits with filter and coil cleaning, refrigerant, electrical and controls checks.
  • Chillers, AHUs & FCUs: servicing of central plant, air handling and fan coil units.
  • Ventilation & extract: AHU, fresh-air, kitchen and car-park exhaust systems.
  • 24/7 emergency breakdown cover: priority response for critical cooling.
  • Wider MEP option: electrical and plumbing maintenance under one contract.
  • Reporting & compliance: visit logs, asset condition reports and recommendations.

Buildings we maintain

AGM maintains offices and towers, retail units and malls, hotels and hospitality, warehouses and industrial facilities, clinics and healthcare, schools and mixed-use developments — across Doha, West Bay, Lusail, The Pearl, the Industrial Area and the wider Qatar region.

Flexible contract options

Choose the level of cover that fits your building: a comprehensive AMC where parts and labour are included, a non-comprehensive AMC covering scheduled labour with parts billed separately, or a PPM-only schedule. We size the visit frequency to your equipment's age, criticality and manufacturer requirements.

Why AGM

We are a Doha-based HVAC and MEP contractor with more than a decade of field experience across Qatar's commercial and industrial sectors. Because we also design and install HVAC systems, our maintenance teams understand plant end to end — not just how to swap a filter.
