AGM is a Doha-based HVAC contractor serving all of Qatar — a single partner for HVAC design, air conditioning, VRF, chiller and ventilation installation, and ongoing maintenance, for both residential and commercial projects.

One contractor, the full HVAC lifecycle

Most HVAC problems come from work being split across too many hands — one company designs, another installs, a third maintains, and no one is accountable when something goes wrong. AGM keeps it under one roof. We design the system, size and install it, commission it properly and maintain it afterwards, so there is a single point of responsibility from first drawing to routine service.

What we do

  • HVAC design & consultation: load calculations, system selection and drawings.
  • AC installation: home & villa and central/commercial systems.
  • VRF / VRV systems: zoned, energy-efficient installation for commercial buildings.
  • Chillers & central plant: installation and chiller maintenance.
  • Ventilation: fresh-air, exhaust, kitchen and car-park systems.
  • Maintenance & AMC: home and commercial plans and MEP maintenance.

Residential & commercial

We work for homeowners fitting out a villa and for facility managers running office towers, malls, hotels, warehouses and industrial sites. The engineering discipline is the same; the scope and service level flex to the project.
