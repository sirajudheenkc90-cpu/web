AGM improves indoor air quality across Qatar with better filtration, controlled fresh-air supply, ventilation and humidity management — so buildings that stay sealed against the heat still get clean, healthy air.

Why IAQ matters here

In Qatar's heat, buildings run sealed and recirculate air, trapping dust, allergens, CO2 and odours. Poor IAQ affects health, comfort, sleep and productivity.

What we do

Upgraded filtration, fresh-air (make-up) systems, ventilation and extract, humidity control, and coil/duct cleaning to remove the sources of poor air — matched to your space.

Homes and workplaces

Villas, offices, clinics and schools benefit most. Pairs with ventilation systems and duct cleaning.
