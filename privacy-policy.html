Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AGM | The Cool Consultant ("AGM", "we", "us") handles information collected through our website, www.agmqatar.com. By using our website or submitting an enquiry, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to provide and basic technical data needed to operate and improve the site:

  • Contact details you submit through our forms, calls or WhatsApp — such as your name, phone number, email, company name, project location, project type and any message you send.
  • Usage and device data collected automatically through cookies and analytics tools — such as pages visited, approximate location, browser type, and how you arrived at our site (including via Google Ads).

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, and arrange site surveys.
  • To provide and manage our HVAC services and maintenance contracts.
  • To measure and improve our website and marketing, including advertising performance.
  • To communicate with you about your request via phone, WhatsApp, email or SMS.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking, to understand how visitors use our site and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. These tools may set cookies and collect anonymised usage data. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work correctly if cookies are disabled.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business and website (for example, analytics and communication providers), and where required by law. These providers are expected to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to us.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond to and service your request, and to meet our legal and business record-keeping requirements, after which it is securely deleted.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided to us. To do so, contact us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including serving our website over a secure (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.
