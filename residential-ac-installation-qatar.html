The right cooling for your home

A comfortable home in Qatar starts with the right AC — sized for the room, installed properly and quiet enough to forget it's there. Whether you're fitting out a new villa, replacing tired old units or adding cooling to an extension, AGM handles the whole job: advising on the best unit, sizing it accurately, running the pipework and electrics neatly, and testing everything before we leave.

Systems we install

  • Split AC: wall-mounted units for individual rooms — efficient and easy to service.
  • Ducted & concealed AC: hidden indoor units for a clean, uniform look across a villa.
  • Multi-zone systems: one outdoor unit serving several rooms with independent control.
  • Replacements & upgrades: swapping old, inefficient units for modern energy-saving models.

Sized right, fitted clean

We calculate the correct capacity for each room based on area, ceiling height, sun exposure and use — not a rough guess by square metre. An accurately sized unit cools faster, runs quieter and costs less to run. Our technicians protect your floors and walls, route pipework tidily, and leave the site clean.

Warranty & aftercare

Every installation is backed by warranty, and you can keep your new system in top condition with an AGM home AC maintenance plan or AMC. One call covers installation, servicing and any future support.
