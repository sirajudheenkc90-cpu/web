Why the thermostat matters

A faulty or badly placed thermostat causes short-cycling, uneven temperatures and wasted energy. The right one, placed and configured well, keeps rooms steady and cuts run time.

Smart & programmable options

Programmable and smart thermostats let you schedule cooling around occupancy — real savings over Qatar's long season — with app control and away modes.

Correct wiring and setup

We match the thermostat to your equipment, wire it safely, and configure schedules and limits. Related: thermostat causes of short-cycling.
