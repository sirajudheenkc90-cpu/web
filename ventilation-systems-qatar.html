AGM designs, installs and maintains commercial and industrial ventilation systems across Qatar — from fresh-air supply and general exhaust to commercial kitchen hoods, car-park ventilation and smoke extract — all sized to code and commissioned to the design airflow.

Ventilation that meets code and comfort

Good ventilation is about more than comfort — in Qatar it's a code and safety requirement. Kitchens need grease-rated extract, car parks need contaminant removal, and occupied spaces need enough fresh air to stay healthy. AGM designs each system to the applicable standards, sizes the fans and ductwork correctly, and commissions it so the airflow on site matches the airflow on the drawings.

Systems we design & install

  • Fresh-air & supply ventilation: treated fresh air for offices, retail and public spaces.
  • General & toilet exhaust: extract systems for occupied and service areas.
  • Commercial kitchen exhaust: canopy hoods, grease filtration and make-up air.
  • Car-park ventilation: ducted and jet-fan systems for CO and contaminant control.
  • Smoke extract & pressurisation: life-safety ventilation and stairwell pressurisation.
  • Industrial fans & ducting: process extract and heavy-duty fan systems.

Design, install, balance

We handle airflow calculations and fan selection, ductwork fabrication and installation, controls and interlocks, and full commissioning with testing and air balancing — supported by handover reports for compliance and facilities teams.

Maintenance & cleaning

Fans, filters and ducting need regular attention to stay efficient and compliant. AGM maintains ventilation systems as a standalone service or within a wider commercial HVAC maintenance contract, and works alongside our VRF and cooling installations for a complete building solution.
