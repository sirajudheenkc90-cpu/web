AGM designs and installs commercial VRF/VRV air conditioning systems across Qatar — handling load calculation, capacity sizing, equipment selection, installation and commissioning for offices, hotels, retail and mixed-use buildings that need zoned, energy-efficient cooling.

Why VRF for commercial buildings

In a multi-tenant or multi-zone building, occupancy and heat gains vary constantly — a boardroom fills up while a corridor sits empty. VRF meets that reality by modulating refrigerant flow to each indoor unit independently, and heat-recovery systems can even shift heat from a warm zone to one that needs it. The result is precise comfort with markedly lower running costs than banks of fixed-speed split units — a decisive advantage in Qatar's climate, where cooling dominates the energy bill.

Capacity & sectors served

  • Offices & towers: floor-by-floor zoning with centralised control.
  • Hotels & hospitality: individual guest-room control with quiet operation.
  • Retail & F&B: flexible cooling for varied trading hours and loads.
  • Clinics & mixed-use: independent zones for different tenants and functions.
  • Large villas & compounds: whole-property comfort from compact plant.

Design, install & commission

We manage the full project lifecycle: a room-by-room cooling load calculation, outdoor/indoor unit selection within pipe-length and diversity limits, refrigerant pipework and drainage, electrical and controls integration, and full commissioning with airflow balancing and performance verification. You receive a system sized to the building — not to a rule of thumb.

Efficiency, controls & maintenance

VRF pairs naturally with centralised or BMS controls for scheduling, monitoring and energy management. To protect efficiency and warranty over the system's life, AGM offers ongoing cover — see commercial HVAC maintenance and AMC plans.
